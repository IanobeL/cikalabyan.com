Fire Evacuation Simulation & Egress Analysis

Mardi Rahayu Hospital, Kudus · PT Global Rancang Selaras

I model how everyone in a multi-storey hospital gets out during a fire, including patients who cannot walk. I find the bottlenecks and check that the building empties well before conditions become dangerous. This is a weekly-reported study for the firm's principal.

530occupants modelled floor by floor, with profiles
RSET < ASETwith a 1.5 safety factor
1:1 · 4:1staff per wheelchair · staff per stretcher
40 mmax travel distance, sprinklered healthcare

What I did

  • Built the occupant workbook (area per person, staff ratios, patient types) and imported the floor plans into Pathfinder.
  • Used Steering mode for assisted evacuation, with walking speeds of 1.0 m/s for staff and 0.6 m/s for elderly people.
  • Ran the evacuations, read the congestion at stairs and doors, and reported results as videos and slides.

Standards

  • SNI 03-1746-2000 (means of egress)
  • Permen PU 26/2008 (fire protection)
  • Defend-in-place: 2.8 m² per patient

Acoustics · measurement software

Room Acoustic Measurement & Microphone Calibration

KAMI, a personal project

A microphone in a small room records a coloured sound: the room's resonances boost some frequencies and the mic adds its own curve. KAMI takes a REW frequency-response measurement, finds the peaks and nulls, and writes an EQ correction profile that flattens the response.

What I built

  • A database that stores every raw measurement by content hash, so each export traces back to its source.
  • Two correction algorithms (wide-peak cut, peak/null mirror) with gains limited to −6 to +6 dB.
  • A local web interface for comparing rooms and microphones.

Verification

  • 20 unit tests passing.
  • Reproduces the earlier script outputs byte for byte, including rounding behaviour.

Energy · climate-responsive design

Building Energy Analysis

Climate Consultant · Rhino 8 + Ladybug · EDGE

I read the site's climate first, test the building form against sun and daylight, and then check the savings against a green-building baseline. Each step's output becomes a design decision rather than a report appendix.

Analysis → output

  • Climate Consultant: weather data to a psychrometric chart and a ranked list of passive strategies (shading, natural ventilation, thermal mass).
  • Ladybug on Rhino 8: sun path, solar radiation on façades, and daylight levels inside the rooms.
  • EDGE: projected energy, water and embodied-materials savings against the local baseline.

Output → design solution

  • Building orientation that reduces east and west exposure.
  • Shading depth sized from the solar angles.
  • Window-to-wall ratio balanced between daylight and heat gain.
  • Glazing and envelope choices fed back into EDGE for the final check.

JLPT Mock-Exam Platform

Kebun Academy · built for my own Japanese classes

My students were training for jobs in Japan and needed real exam practice, so I built a full JLPT simulator. It has a hidden teacher dashboard and a separate tool for writing questions. Students take timed tests, I see where they fail, and I add questions on exactly those points.

1,000N4–N3 questions in the bank
4 monthsbeginner to N3 level, Batch 2
110 → 120mock score out of 180, month 1 to month 2
3 appsstudent app, question tool, API

Features

  • Timed N5–N1 tests with reading passages and a listening audio player.
  • Results with radar charts, per-section minimum-score checks and a pass certificate.
  • Customizable teacher dashboard: test settings, student history, question publishing.
  • Question tool that converts photographed exam papers into the bank format in batches.

Architecture

  • The two front-end apps run on Google Cloud Run.
  • A separate Node.js API is the only part that holds database credentials.
  • MongoDB Atlas stores the question bank, exam histories and settings.
