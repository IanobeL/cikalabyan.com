KAMI: Room Acoustic Measurement & Calibration

Personal project · working name RoomTune

A microphone in a small room records a coloured sound: the room's resonances boost some frequencies and the mic adds its own curve. KAMI takes a REW frequency-response measurement, finds the peaks and nulls, and writes an EQ correction profile that flattens the response. The approach is conservative and cut-first: it removes problem peaks before it adds anything back.

What I built

  • A database that stores every raw measurement by content hash, so each export traces back to its source.
  • Two correction algorithms (wide-peak cut, peak/null mirror) with gains limited to −6 to +6 dB.
  • A local web interface for comparing rooms and microphones.

Verification

  • 20 unit tests passing.
  • Reproduces the earlier script outputs byte for byte, including rounding behaviour.

Who it is for

Home studios and creators who need to understand their room, not only export a curve: measure it, explain what the room is doing, recommend a conservative fix, and show the before and after.

Next

  • Package it as a desktop app; real-time audio comes later.
